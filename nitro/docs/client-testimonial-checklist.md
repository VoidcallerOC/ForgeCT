# FORGE CT client testimonial checklist

**Current status:** NOT STARTED / BLOCKED on owner-approved quotes. No first-party Forge CT testimonial with approved public attribution exists in the repository. `src/components/client-notes.tsx` stays empty and renders nothing until real client wording and permission are available.

**Owner (Nick) — exact asks before publish:**

1. Ask 2–3 completed-build clients for a short note (email reply is enough).
2. Get explicit permission to publish quote + name + business name on forge-ct.com.
3. Paste only approved wording into `CLIENT_NOTES` in `src/components/client-notes.tsx` (never invent or polish into a claim they did not make).
4. Do not add star ratings, revenue, or conversion claims.

## Collect two or three strong notes

Ask clients who have completed a build about the parts of working together that they can speak to directly:

- What did the business need its website to help customers understand or do?
- What was it like working directly with Nick from first conversation through launch?
- What felt thoughtful, responsive, or specific to the business?
- What do customers or staff find easier to use on the finished site? Include this only if the client can describe it accurately.

A short email reply or a brief recorded conversation that the client approves is enough. Do not write a quote for the client or turn a prompt into a claim they did not make.

## Before publishing each quote

- Preserve the client's exact wording; send any edited version back for approval.
- Confirm the approved name, business name, and role (if used).
- Get explicit permission to publish the quote and attribution on the Forge CT website.
- Confirm whether permission covers a live link to the business website.
- Record the approval alongside the final copy so future edits do not exceed it.
- Do not add star ratings, conversion figures, revenue claims, or other outcomes unless separately verified and explicitly approved.

## Display standard

Use a concise quote, the approved attribution, and no rating-wall treatment. Keep the quote area hidden until at least one real quote is ready; prefer two or three particularly useful notes over a larger collection of weaker ones.

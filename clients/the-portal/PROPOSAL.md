## Three options (proposed values, for Nick to approve)

Prices follow Forge CT's published rate sheet: Basic $750 / Standard $1,500 / Premium $2,500 / Care $35 a month.

### Option A: Standard paid build ($2,500, Premium tier)

- The demo, finished and launched on theportalct.com: 7 pages plus a 404 page.
- Owner review round: hours, events, photos and wording confirmed by the store.
- Event list the store can update (simple data file, or Facebook/Google Calendar embed, whichever the owner prefers).
- Domain cutover from Weebly, Google Business Profile check, sitemap, analytics.
- 2 revision rounds. Launch target 10 business days after content sign-off.
- **Payment:** 50% to start, 50% at launch.
- Optional Care at $35 a month (hours, events and copy updates).

### Option B: Cash + store credit (hybrid)

- Same scope as Option A.
- **$1,500 cash** (50% to start, 50% at launch) **+ $1,250 in store credit/merchandise at retail value.**
- The credit premium (+$250) reflects that credit costs the store less than its face value.
- Care can be traded too: **$50 a month in store credit** in place of $35 cash.
- Credit terms to agree in writing: issued at launch, non-expiring (or a 24-month minimum), usable on any in-stock item, and the redemption method (gift card or store account).

### Option C: Trade-only, limited scope

- **3 pages:** Home, Events and Visit (Basic tier scope). Card/Board/RPG content goes into Home sections. No quiz.
- **$1,000 in store credit at retail value**, issued 50% at start and 50% at launch.
- 1 revision round. The store supplies or approves all content.
- No Care included. Updates after launch are billed or traded separately.
- Can be upgraded to the full build later by paying the difference at Option A rates.

### What's never included without a separate quote

E-commerce or online inventory, a real-time singles/stock database, tournament software or registration payments, paid ads, or photography beyond the store's existing photos.

## Before quoting (owner must confirm)

1. Current hours. The demo uses the hours from the Weebly Contact page, which may be out of date.
2. Event days and times for FNM, board game days, minis days and learn-to-play RPG sessions. The demo labels these "Schedule TBA".

4. Permission to use the store's photos and logo. The demo uses the photos from the store's current site.
5. Who controls the theportalct.com domain and the Weebly account.
6. The "ten years" claim on the old site, which is now out of date. The demo avoids stating the store's age.

## Guardrails for Nick

- **Do not accept or promise a deal on the spot.** Collect the owner's preferred option and confirm it in writing afterward.
- **Use a written agreement** covering scope, payment or credit schedule, credit terms, ownership (the site transfers to the store on final payment), and what counts as a revision. For anything unusual in the trade terms, have a CT attorney review the agreement. I'm not acting as your lawyer.
- **Barter is taxable income.** Store credit received for services is generally reportable at fair market value. Check with a tax preparer.
- The demo is labelled "Concept preview, not the official site" and set to `noindex`. Keep it that way until the owner approves launch.

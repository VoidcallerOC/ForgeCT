The space

Not a shop with a table. A place to play.

The Portal has brought every kind of tabletop game to Manchester — and the room to play them.

  • Large gaming space with seventeen tables and terrain for every scale of battle.
  • Game library of more than five hundred titles you can play in store.
  • Special orders — if it's in print and not on the shelf, we'll order it for you.
  • Host your own RPG group, miniatures day or board game night. Just ask the staff.

New to card games?

You might be a card gamer if…

The store's own guide says it simply: if you enjoy collecting as part of a hobby, and you enjoy the challenge of playing against an opponent, CCGs are for you.

  • Start with a deck, not a binder. Staff can point you to a good first purchase.
  • Come to a Friday. It's the fastest way to learn and meet people.
  • Can't find a product? If it's in print, the store will order it in.

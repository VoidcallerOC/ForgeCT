Where does my card go?

To Stripe, on their page, not this one. FORGE never sees or stores a card number.

How do I cancel care?

The link above opens your own billing page. Cancel there and the plan stops at the end of the month you paid for. You do not have to ask me first.

Can I pay by check?

For a build deposit, yes. Ask and I will send it as a bank transfer invoice or take a check. Care plans need a card or bank account on file so the month does not turn into an invoice chase.

What happens if a payment fails?

Stripe retries it and emails you. The page stays up while that sorts itself out. Nothing goes dark over an expired card.
